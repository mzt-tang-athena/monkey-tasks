## Spec

Ship 1.4 through `scripts/release.sh`, as 1.3 went.

1.4 carries:

- Deeds read as tasks, and a Board tab grouping them by status.
- A Today list that leads the tabs, with a Past group above today.
- One normal reminder by default, with an Alarm switch per task.
- Three reminder buttons: Done, Snooze and Didn't do. Done and
  Didn't do work with the app killed.
- A search bar and a one or two column choice in the Inbox header.
- One row for the capture sheet's list, link and Send controls.
- A tap on the planner's split handle hides the task half.
- A cold start that reads notes side by side and shows last
  session's rows, dimmed, at once.
- Swipe right on Add for the next tab; shorter Day, Week, Month and
  form toggles.
- TalkBack hears the checked theme, and the task tick.

The version becomes 1.4.0, and the Android version code becomes 5.
Land the bump and the dated changelog through a PR first.
