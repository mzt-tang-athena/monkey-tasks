## Spec

The bottom bar and the header bands draw their glyphs small.
The tabs draw 20pt and the split pill 18pt.
The Lists band draws its sort and overflow marks at 20pt.
The operator decided the chrome glyphs grow to 24pt.
Every touch target stays 44pt, so the glyph fills more of its box.

A small glyph inside a row or a chip grows 2pt.
That covers the capture chips and their marks.
It also covers the section and source chevrons and the picker check.
The settings row chevrons, the sheet close, the bell and the note
marks follow.
The form toggle and the editor's property marks grow too.

The sizes live in the generator's table, `app/scripts/icons/build.ts`,
and the generated `app/src/components/ui/glyphs.ts`.

Four tabs, the plus and command pill and the swipe gap must still fit
360dp.
Rows, chips, the editor's property buttons and the planner header must
show no clipping and no misalignment.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Six bar glyphs draw at 24pt inside their 44pt targets.
- [ ] The Lists band's sort and overflow draw at 24pt.
- [ ] Every small row and chip glyph draws 2pt over its old box.
- [ ] Four tabs, the pill and the gap fit 360dp.
- [ ] Rows, chips, the editor's properties and the planner header show
      no clipping or misalignment at 360 and 412.
- [ ] Before and after shots at 360 and 412 in two palettes.
- [ ] The gallery, axe and full web suite pass.
